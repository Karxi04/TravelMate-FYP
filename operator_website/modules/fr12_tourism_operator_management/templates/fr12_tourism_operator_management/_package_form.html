Authoritative Currency: MYR
How Pricing Models Work in TravelMate:
👤 Per Person (per_person): Each traveller is charged individually: (Adult Price × adults) + (Child Price × children). Recommended for open walking tours and group excursions.
👥 Per Group (per_group): One flat fixed fee charged for the entire private party, regardless of size up to your maximum capacity. Recommended for private charters and private docent tours.

Catalog Availability vs Administrator Moderation
  • Available: Eligible for Traveller discovery after Admin approval. Once approved, the package is immediately active and open for bookings in the mobile app.
